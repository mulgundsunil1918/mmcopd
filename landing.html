✨ What you get

Everything a busy hospital needs. 🏥

From the moment a patient walks in to the printed slip — every workflow is built in. No third-party plugins, no monthly fees, no surprises.

👥 Reception & Appointments

Search by name / phone / UHID, register in 30 seconds, book slots with double-booking prevention, and send WhatsApp confirmations.

🩺 Doctor Consultation

Vitals strip, prescription with drug auto-complete, and fully custom OPD slip templates per specialty. Per-doctor quick-fill templates, and a live pediatric growth section that plots centiles as you type.

💊 Pharmacy with FEFO

Batch-tracked inventory with mandatory manufacture & expiry dates, First-Expired-First-Out dispensing, Schedule H register, purchase invoices, low-stock + expiry alerts.

📊 Analytics Dashboard

Revenue trends, demographics, geography, retention cohorts, doctor-wise performance, and operational reports — all built in.

🖨️ Bilingual OPD Slip

Beautiful A4 print with clinic letterhead, vitals, Rx table, and a follow-up offer in English + Kannada. Customizable per specialty.

🔒 Your Data, Your Control

Zero-cloud architecture with daily auto-backup, USB reminders, and one-click restore. Staff logins are role-based — receptionist, doctor, lab, pharmacy — with hashed passwords and a full audit trail of who changed what.

🌐 Multi-Station over your LAN

Run reception, each doctor's cabin, and the pharmacy counter on separate PCs sharing one database. Pair a station with a 6-character code — no IP typing. Wired or Wi-Fi, with built-in diagnostics and automatic reconnect.

💬 WhatsApp for Patients

One click sends an appointment confirmation — you review it before it goes. Connect the official WhatsApp Business API to add scheduled campaigns, follow-up reminders, a shared reply inbox, and AI-drafted replies.

🧪 Laboratory — 170+ tests ready

Load a standard catalog of ~115 pathology + ~55 radiology investigations in one click, set your own prices, enable only what you offer. Order from the consult, enter results, print reports, and bill in a tap.

🛏️ In-Patient (IPD)

Colour-coded ward & bed map, doctor→reception admission requests, running bill with advances, vitals · MAR · nursing notes · intake/output · diet, transfers, MLC register, and discharge with outcomes (LAMA/DAMA/death/referred).

🍼 Pediatrics add-on

Growth centiles on WHO (0–5y) and IAP 2015 (5–18y) charts, immunisation diary from IAP/NIS schedules, and calculators — mid-parental height, corrected age. Prints on your letterhead or sends to reception.

🛡️ Insurance / TPA (cashless)

Insurer master, pre-authorisation tracking, and the full claim lifecycle — claimed, approved, settled — reconciled against the patient's bill, with the pipeline shown in analytics.

📄 Discharge Summary builder

Build a summary from your own templated sections, save and re-edit it, then preview & print on the clinic letterhead — the same header as your bills and OPD slips.

🔐 Who sees what

Role-based access, out of the box.

Every staff member logs in with their own password. What they can open is decided by their role — so reception can't change clinical notes, and a lab tech only sees the lab. The admin controls it all.
